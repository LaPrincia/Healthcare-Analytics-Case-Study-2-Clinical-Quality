# 🏥 Clinical Quality Performance & Population Health
### A Healthcare Analytics Case Study 2

## Project Overview

This healthcare analytics case study demonstrates how clinical quality and population health data can be transformed into actionable insights using Microsoft SQL Server and Power BI.

Developed for the fictional Palmetto Regional Health System (PRHS), this project analyzes clinical quality performance, patient risk, preventive screening, care gaps, and 30-day readmissions to support executive decision-making, improve healthcare quality, and identify opportunities for performance improvement.

---

## Project Highlights

- Developed SQL queries to support executive clinical quality reporting
- Validated KPI calculations to ensure dashboard accuracy
- Built interactive Power BI dashboards for Clinical Quality Performance and Population Health
- Designed executive KPI scorecards to monitor quality performance and patient risk
- Applied healthcare performance improvement principles to identify opportunities for quality improvement and population health management

---

## Business Challenge

Palmetto Regional Health System (PRHS) sought greater visibility into clinical quality performance and population health metrics to better identify care gaps, monitor preventive screening compliance, evaluate patient risk, and reduce avoidable readmissions.

Executive leadership required a comprehensive reporting solution capable of transforming patient-level clinical data into actionable insights that support quality improvement, population health management, and strategic decision-making.
